How do I get my Strava export?
  1. On strava.com open Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Get Started.
  2. Under Download Request click Request your archive. Strava emails a link, usually within a few hours.
  3. Drop the downloaded ZIP above. It can be large (photos are included); only activities.csv and the files in activities/ are read.

Cycling only for now: runs and other sports are counted and skipped. Garmin .fit files are supported; older .gpx/.tcx rides are listed as skipped.
